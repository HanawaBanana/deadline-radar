Build With AI: Basics
Hosted by Devpost

Bring an idea to life and learn the basics of building with AI. This beginner friendly
hackathon runs in 2026 and is open to everyone, whether you write code every day or you are
opening a terminal for the first time.

Submission window
Submissions open September 22, 2026 at 10:00 AM PT and close October 26, 2026 at 5:00pm ET.
There is no entry fee and no purchase necessary.

What you need to submit
Every submission must include a public GitHub repository and a 1 to 3 minute demo video.
The repository must contain scope.md, prd.md and spec.md, written during the event.
Submit your project on the Devpost page before the deadline; late entries are not judged.

Prizes
Grand Prize: $5,000 USD
Second place: $2,000 USD
Third place: $1,000 USD

Judging
Judging runs November 2, 2026 through November 16, 2026. Projects are scored on design,
innovation and presentation. Winners announced December 1, 2026.
